# Build 132 — Independent Continuity Recurrence Recheck, Overdue Exception Resolution & Verified Closure Follow-Up

- Authorized finance owners at **both campgrounds** can record append-only independent continuity rechecks against the latest Build 131 reopened-exception aging review. The first recheck is unique, and subsequent records must advance along one ordered chain.
- Review stages are independent recurrence recheck → overdue evidence resolution → verified closure follow-up. Pending and escalated outcomes may be reconsidered, but verified stages cannot be skipped. A different reviewer must record each consecutive stage, and the initial reviewer cannot be the owner or Build 131 aging reviewer.
- Verified stage outcomes require the same-reopening, newer-than-owner-reopening, independently verified Build 130 follow-up receipt and its linked owner evidence. Unverified stages cannot attach proof. Outcomes with an open due date must be due within 90 days; overdue evidence of 30+ days requires explicit escalation or verified proof.
- Final verified closure follow-up requires verified prior evidence resolution, no open recurrence peers, no unresolved owner dispositions and no recent owner escalation. Its receipt has no open due date and does **not** remove operational holds, change payments, reservations, or any customer data.
- The restricted owner UI shows the live follow-up queue, independent outcome and source-proof selectors, due dates, observed exception counts, and append-only audit history. RLS is forced and `icamp_app` can only read/insert.
- Release criteria: Prettier, ESLint, TypeScript, full tests including Build 132, production Next.js build, migration `0162` and verification SQL, Postgres integration and browser E2E, GitHub CI/security checks.
