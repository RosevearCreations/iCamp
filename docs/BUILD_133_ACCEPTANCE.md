# Build 133 — Verified Closure Recurrence Monitoring, Independent Evidence Renewal & Overdue Exception Reopening Audit

- Build 133 begins only after a **verified Build 132 closure follow-up**, with linked Build 130 evidence and matching source/target campgrounds. Independent auditors record recurrence monitoring, evidence renewal and overdue reopening audits in one append-only chain.
- Independent reviewers must differ from the verified source reviewer and the previous audit reviewer. A unique initial monitor and unique prior-record link prevent competing monitoring branches. Stored metrics capture proof age, prior overdue age, open peer recurrences, unresolved owner decisions and recent escalations.
- Stable monitoring cannot ignore unresolved peer or owner exceptions. More than 30 days overdue cannot silently remain ordinary stable monitoring or an un-escalated evidence request. Reopening audits require a documented overdue condition, escalated renewal or unresolved exception.
- A **verified owner reopening** must cite a real same-pair Build 130 `owner_exception_reopen` event newer than the verified closure and follow a recorded `owner_reopening_required` decision. Pending audits cannot claim an actual reopening. No audit record itself releases a hold, creates operational reservations, transfers money or edits guest information.
- Restricted owner forms offer stage-specific outcomes, due dates, evidence references, eligible reopening IDs and chronological receipts. Forced RLS and `icamp_app` read/insert-only permissions apply to the new table.
- The supplied Rosevear Integration Contract v1 remains a future integration boundary: iCamp retains ownership of its campground records; no direct cross-application database access or external write command is introduced in Build 133.
- Release gates: Prettier, TypeScript, ESLint, full tests including Build 133, optimized Next.js build, migration `0163` plus verification SQL, PostgreSQL lifecycle/idempotence, browser E2E, CodeQL, Secret Scan, and confirmed `dev`/`main` status.
